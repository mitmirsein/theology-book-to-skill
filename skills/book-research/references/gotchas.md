# Failure modes

## Do not confuse claim states

generated is a model-produced candidate, not evidence. source-checked requires a real locator and rereading of the cited source segment. Do not assign human-reviewed without source-checked evidence.

## Do not overstate authorial relations

Shared tags do not establish agreement, influence, or criticism between authors. Keep claim IDs and attribution in every comparison. Label the researcher's own connection as basis=inferred.

## Do not overtrust locators

A PDF page index can differ from printed pagination. An EPUB spine item is not a screen page. When the source file is unavailable or locator reliability is low, report the limit rather than implying verification.

## Do not force a comparison

One relevant book does not support a multi-author synthesis. State the limit and identify what further sources would be needed.
